Career Guidance Talk

Date: November 10, 2026

Time: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Venue: Seminar Hall

Category: Career Development

About the Event

The Career Guidance Talk is designed to help students understand current industry expectations, emerging career opportunities, and professional growth strategies. Experts from various industries will share practical advice and real-world experiences.

Participants will gain insights into resume building, interview preparation, higher education opportunities, and career planning. The session aims to help students make informed decisions about their future careers.
