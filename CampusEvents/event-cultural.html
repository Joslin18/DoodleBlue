About the Event

Cultural Day is a vibrant celebration of traditions, creativity, and artistic talent. Students participate in dance, music, drama, and other cultural performances that showcase diversity and unity.

The event provides a platform for students to express themselves, appreciate different cultures, and enjoy entertaining performances while building meaningful connections with fellow participants.
