Photography Contest

Date: November 5, 2026

Time: 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM

Venue: Media Studio

Category: Cultural Event

About the Event

The Photography Contest provides students with an opportunity to showcase their creativity, storytelling skills, and artistic vision through the lens of a camera. Participants can submit photographs based on various themes and categories.

The competition encourages innovation, attention to detail, and visual communication. Winning entries will be displayed in a campus exhibition and recognized with exciting prizes.
