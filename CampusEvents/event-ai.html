About the Event

The AI Workshop introduces students to the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and emerging technologies that are transforming the digital world. Participants will gain practical exposure through demonstrations and guided activities.

Industry professionals and faculty experts will share insights into real-world AI applications, career opportunities, and current trends. The workshop is ideal for students interested in technology, innovation, and future-ready skills.
