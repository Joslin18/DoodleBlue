Inter-College Sports Meet

Date: October 25, 2026

Time: 9:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Venue: College Sports Ground

Category: Sports Event

About the Event

The Inter-College Sports Meet brings together talented athletes from various colleges to compete in a range of sporting events. Participants demonstrate teamwork, discipline, and determination while competing in a healthy environment.

The event includes athletics, football, volleyball, cricket, and other popular sports. It encourages physical fitness, sportsmanship, and the development of leadership skills among students.
